Why GreenShift

The trust boundary

Every operator knows total site consumption down to the watt. Which tenant caused it? Nobody knows — measurement stops at the rack and PDU, and visibility into tenants' guest systems is off-limits for good reason. That's exactly the boundary that EU energy labelling requirements (EnEfG) and your customers' CSRD sustainability reports now require you to cross. Estimates don't hold up to audit; flat-rate allocation cross-subsidizes inefficient tenants.

In September 2026 the EU adopted a mandatory energy and water efficiency labelling scheme for data centres of 500 kW and above, with the first labels due in August 2027.

Two measurement layers, one reconciliation

GreenShift combines a physical reference measurement at the PDU with relative load shares from a software agent — and continuously reconciles the two. The result: attribution per tenant and workload based on actual measurements, not modelling assumptions.

What this means for you: audit-ready, customer-level energy disclosure from live measurement data instead of an annual one-off analysis; the end of cross-subsidy, since consumption is billed to whoever causes it, and can be passed through to tenants to refinance the service itself; and a tenant portal and reports that can be white-labelled in your own branding — GreenShift stays the technology supplier in the background.
